CropDoctor

Drop in a photo of a leaf, fruit or stem. CropDoctor names the plant and lists the diseases it most resembles.

Ready when you are

Any image format works, including iPhone HEIC. Close-ups of the affected area give the best results.

    CropDoctor is a guide, not a diagnosis. Its training data has no healthy-plant category, so it can't confirm that a plant is healthy. For treatment decisions, ask a local agronomist.
